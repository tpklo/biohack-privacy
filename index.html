Biohack Beauty — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Biohack Beauty content-publishing tool (the “App”) collects, uses, and protects information when it publishes content to social media accounts that we own and operate for the Biohack Beauty brand.

1. Who we are

Biohack Beauty is a skincare and beauty brand. The App is an internal tool used by our team to research, create, and schedule our own marketing content across our own official social media accounts (Facebook, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, and LinkedIn).

2. What the App does

The App helps our team draft posts and publishes approved content to our own brand-owned social media accounts via each platform’s official API. The App does not interact with, collect data from, or post on behalf of any other users.

3. Information we access

  • Access tokens for our own Pages/accounts, used solely to publish our content.
  • Content we create (captions, images, videos) and its publishing status.
  • Basic post analytics of our own posts (e.g. reach, engagement) where provided by the platform.

The App does not collect personal data of end users, followers, or any third parties. We do not buy, sell, or share access tokens or content with third parties.

4. How we use the information

  • To publish and schedule our own marketing content.
  • To review the performance of our own posts.

5. Data storage & retention

Access tokens and content are stored securely on systems controlled by our team and are retained only as long as needed to operate the App. Tokens are revocable at any time from each platform’s settings.

6. Third-party platforms

The App uses the official APIs of Meta (Facebook, Instagram, Threads), TikTok, and LinkedIn. Your use of those platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Deletion

Because the App only processes our own brand’s accounts and content, no end-user personal data is collected. To request deletion of any data the App holds about our accounts, or to revoke the App’s access:

  • Revoke the App’s access from the platform’s connected-apps / business settings, and/or
  • Email us at rada@rittandso.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request”.

We will delete all stored tokens and related content data within 30 days of a verified request.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy: rada@rittandso.com
